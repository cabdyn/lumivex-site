Lumivex Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · applies to the Lumivex app for Android

The short version

Lumivex does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (solved puzzles, stars, unfinished puzzles, daily puzzle results and streak) and your settings (sound switches) in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device, is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

Lumivex asks for no special permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it has no internet permission.

Sharing a daily result

When you tap Share after a daily puzzle, the game copies a short text (date, stars, time, hint and mistake markers) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere; you decide whether to paste it.

Children

Lumivex collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data (for example an online leaderboard, purchases or optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here with a new effective date.
