Lumivex Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · applies to the Lumivex app for Android

The short version

Lumivex itself does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only exceptions are the optional Google Play Games feature (achievements and leaderboards) and the Google Play age check, both run by Google and described below.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (solved puzzles, stars, unfinished puzzles, daily puzzle results and streak) and your settings (sound switches) in the app's private storage on your device. This information is never sent to the developer, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. If you use Google Play Games (below), the achievements and scores worked out from it are sent to Google.

Permissions

Lumivex asks for no special permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. It uses the internet only for the optional Google Play Games feature and the Google Play age check; the game itself works fully offline.

Google Play Games (version 1.1 and later)

Lumivex can use Google Play Games Services to show achievements and leaderboards. This is optional: the game works fully, including offline, without it.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) with the game, and stores your unlocked achievements and leaderboard scores (your daily streak and total stars) on Google's servers. Your gamer name and scores can be shown to other players on the leaderboards, depending on your Play Games profile settings. The Play Games software also sends Google diagnostic and usage data to keep the service working. This data is encrypted in transit.

The developer of Lumivex does not run any servers and does not receive your email address or any other personal data through this feature. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

You can see and delete your Play Games data, for this game or entirely, in your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Google Play age check (version 1.2 and later)

Some regions have laws that require apps to ask the app store for a user's age range. To follow these laws, Lumivex uses the Google Play Age Signals API when it starts. It first asks Google Play whether these rules apply to you. If they don't, the check stops there and nothing else is requested. If they do, Google Play may ask you to agree to share an age signal, and may then tell the game your age range or whether you are verified as an adult.

Lumivex does not save this information, does not send it to the developer or anyone else, and does not use it for ads or tracking. The game is suitable for all ages and works the same whatever the answer is. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Lumivex keeps no data about you on any server of the developer, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Your data can be deleted in two places:

Achievements and leaderboard scores (Google Play Games)

  1. Open your Play Games profile (or the Play Games section of your Google Play profile on your phone).
  2. Go to Settings and choose Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Delete the data for Lumivex only, or your whole Play Games account.

This deletes your Lumivex achievements and leaderboard scores from Google's servers. Google handles the deletion under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer keeps no copy.

Progress on your phone

Uninstall Lumivex, or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Lumivex → Storage → Clear storage). This deletes your solved puzzles, stars, daily results and settings. If you are still signed in to Play Games and play again, the game sends your new achievements and scores again.

Questions or help with deletion: moldsoftsupport@gmail.com.

Sharing a daily result

When you tap Share after a daily puzzle, the game copies a short text (date, stars, time, hint and mistake markers) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere; you decide whether to paste it.

Children

Lumivex itself collects no data from anyone, including children. Google Play Games is provided by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data (for example an online leaderboard, purchases or optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here with a new effective date.
